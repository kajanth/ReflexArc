## 2025-02-18 - Concurrent Async Execution over Sequential Wait
**Learning:** Performing multiple independent blocking operations (like network socket timeouts) in a synchronous loop blocks the async event loop and increases total wait time to O(N).
**Action:** Always wrap independent synchronous blocking operations (such as `socket.create_connection`) in `asyncio.to_thread` and execute them concurrently using `asyncio.gather` to reduce the total delay to O(1) (the duration of the slowest single operation).
